How it works

Three steps.
Zero stress.

Step 01

📱

Sign up with your number

No passwords. No paperwork. Enter your Ghana phone number, verify with a one-time SMS code and you are in. Works for both landlords and tenants.

Step 02

🏠

Find a room or list yours

Tenants browse verified available rooms with real pricing. Landlords list properties and manage all their rooms and tenants from a single dashboard.

Step 03

💳

Pay or collect instantly

Tap to pay rent with MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash or AirtelTigo. Landlords get notified the moment money lands. Both parties get a digital receipt.

Features

Everything rent,
in one app.

🔔

Smart rent reminders

CasaPay reminds tenants 7 days, 3 days, 2 days, and the day before rent is due. Then every 2 days after if it is still unpaid.

🪪

Ghana Card verification

Tenants verify their identity with their Ghana Card. Landlords see verified badges before approving any room request.

📊

Landlord dashboard

See all your properties, occupancy rates, and payment status at a glance. Know exactly what is paid and what is pending.

🧾

Instant receipts

Every payment generates a digital receipt automatically. No more arguments about who paid what and when.

🔑

Room requests

Tenants request rooms directly through the app. Landlords approve or decline with one tap. Clean, digital and traceable.

🇬🇭

Built for Ghana

Mobile Money native. Ghana Card compatible. Low-bandwidth optimised. Designed from scratch for how Ghanaians actually live and pay.

About CasaPay

Built by a Ghanaian,
for Ghana.

CasaPay started at Ashesi University. We saw the friction between landlords and tenants every day — missed payments, no receipts, zero transparency. We decided to fix it.

Starting with university hostels and growing across Accra. The mission is simple: make rent painless for everyone involved.
